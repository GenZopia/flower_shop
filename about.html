Heritage

Our Heritage

At Genzopia Flower Shop, every bouquet begins with a story. For more than two decades, we've created timeless arrangements that have graced embassies, luxury hotels, fine restaurants, and private homes across New York, NY.

Our reputation has been built on elegance, craftsmanship, and meticulous attention to detail — qualities that define every stem we arrange and every event we design for.

Philosophy

Our Philosophy

We believe flowers are more than decorations — they are ways to celebrate, connect, and express emotion. That philosophy guides every arrangement we design, from an intimate bridal bouquet to a large-scale diplomatic event.

By sourcing seasonally from trusted growers worldwide, we ensure each design is as fresh, vibrant, and unique as the moment it represents.

The People

Our Team of Designers

Our team blends artistry with innovation, carrying forward traditions of floral design while embracing modern techniques. Many designers have been with Genzopia for years, contributing to the consistency and trust that generations of New Yorkers have come to expect.

Each member brings a unique sensibility, but a shared commitment: every arrangement should feel personal, purposeful, and extraordinary.
